Global GMTED2010 and SRTM terrain data courtesy of the U.S. Geological Survey.

Tiles: N50E007, N54W002, N55W002. Source URLs and SHA-256 hashes are recorded in src/content/geodata/*-dem.json.
The upstream composite does not label the originating provider per sample; relevant regional and global attributions are retained.

Modifications: cropped to two 32 km square regions, locally projected with latitude-dependent metres per longitude degree, bilinearly resampled to 80 m and rounded to whole metres. Heights are not vertically exaggerated. River and tidal water stages are approximated for the game; they are not hydrographic surveys. Terrain is modern elevation data, not an archival reconstruction of 1900. Buildings, roads, industries, populations and vehicle performance are authored gameplay content.
